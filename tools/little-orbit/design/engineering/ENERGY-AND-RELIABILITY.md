# Energy, friction, accuracy and long life

Status: **design reasoning and comparison; no measured power, accuracy or lifetime**. References and access limitations are in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). This is a browser-art/engineering-study plan, not instructions for constructing pressure equipment.

## Finite energy with a visible source

The people supply work through hand cranks and treadles. One-way couplings collect it without dragging a resting person's hands or feet. A torque limiter/controlled clutch and visible isolation separate the operating station from the moving transmission. Two-hand grips, grounded bracing and recovery use the shared anatomy contract. No child or infant operates the power machinery.

Primary reserve: a visible descending weight drives the time train. The height gauge must correspond to stored potential energy in an engineering study, not rise while discharging. A separate flywheel smooths variable input but loses speed/energy when not replenished. A small gravity remontoire near the escapement isolates periodic display loads as far as its validated mechanism permits. Springs remain allowed where they solve a defined function; minimizing them is a design preference, not a law of accuracy or reliability.

Every branch must show its source, conversion, store, output and loss destination. Optional wind or ambient thermal harvesting would need a defined external source, availability model and conversion loss; “energy from around” never means an unspecified perpetual supply. They are comparison candidates, not free baseline power.

## Hydraulic and air branches

| Candidate | Where energy actually resides | Advantages to investigate | Costs and unresolved details | Decision for first study |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct lifted weight | Gravitational potential | Visible reserve; few conversions; gravity remontoire possible | Space, cord/drum wear, support, gearing friction, refill disturbance | Baseline main reserve. |
| Elastic spring | Elastic strain | Compact reserve or compliant return | Torque variation, fatigue/creep and attachment losses; must be counted | Optional for a specific latch/return or reserve comparison, not silently removed from component counts. |
| Flywheel | Rotational kinetic energy | Input smoothing and brief load support | Bearing/windage loss, containment and changing speed | Short-duration input buffer, not centuries of storage. |
| Weighted-piston hydraulic accumulator | A separate elevated weight moved through a fluid/piston path | Visible hydraulic charge/discharge, approximate constant load over stroke | Seals, stiction, leakage, valves, fluid cleanliness and conversion losses | Baseline hydraulic demonstrator for intermittent display/hoist work, isolated from regulator. Its mass is distinct from the main reserve. |
| Gas-loaded hydraulic accumulator | Compressed gas through bladder/piston | Compact stored-energy alternative | Precharge, permeation, seal/bladder aging and service requirements need manufacturer evidence | Comparison only until source/product review. Fluid alone is not the claimed store. |
| Externally pressurized air pads | Air film supports a load; the supply system consumes energy | Reduced rubbing contact while air is available | Compressor/receiver, drying/filtration, air flow, leaks, noise, support on air loss and startup loss | Optional isolated carriage/bearing experiment, not the core clock's default bearing system. |
| Self-acting air bearing | Film generated by relative motion | Avoids some external supply hardware | Speed/load/start-stop limits; film does not exist at every static condition | Research alternative; cannot be assumed suitable for very slow clock pivots. |

Hydraulic loop concept: crank-driven pump → non-return/isolation block → separate weighted accumulator → controlled actuator or hydraulic motor → reservoir/return/filter → pump. Define a relief/limiting path, position/condition indicators and service access at catalog level, without inventing pressure ratings or vessel construction. Show charge, hold, release, return and isolated states. If an actuator lifts a load, account for that load's energy and any recovery once; neither the return fluid nor pressure is a second free energy source.

Air loop concept: input-driven compressor → receiver/conditioning → controlled air pad or demonstration turbine → exhaust/return as applicable. A turbine extracts work from a pressure/flow drop; it does not both power the compressor and leave net free output. Air support reduces a contact loss by adding a supply load; compare total system energy and service burden. On air loss, a designed backup support takes the load and the affected demonstrator stops. No character enters or holds up the gap.

## What “accurate” must mean

Separate absolute time offset, oscillator frequency/rate error, stability over averaging intervals, display/indexing correctness, calendar correctness and energy autonomy. A governor that limits shaft speed is not automatically an accurate time standard. A remontoire reduces one source of drive variation but cannot remove temperature, amplitude, suspension, gravity, air-pressure or aging effects.

The physical study needs a stated reference and environment, rate calibration, temperature/pressure/tilt sensitivity, load/refill disturbance, reserve-end behavior and uncertainty. Candidate temperature-compensated pendulum materials and low-friction pivots require real data. An evacuated enclosure is another possible experiment with sealing/service costs, not a hidden default solution. No target such as “most accurate mechanical clock ever” is substantiated here. The browser remains as accurate as its correctly configured host time source and uses that current time even if the illustrated mechanism is paused.

## Maintenance-conscious architecture

- Compare protected dry/jewel/rolling contact candidates against externally supplied air on total energy, wear, contamination and replaceability. “Low friction” is not “zero wear,” and a hard/ceramic surface alone does not establish a lifetime.
- Reduce high-speed and constantly loaded rubbing interfaces; use supported shafts, balanced loads and isolated intermittent calendar work. Decide each bearing/lubricant/material pair rather than painting every part with the same ideal metal.
- Document seals, cords, springs, filters, lubrication points, detents and running contacts as service items. Age, contamination, corrosion, creep and lubricant change matter even when gear cycles are few.
- Make the timing core, input station, pressure demonstrators, calendar and readout replaceable modules with accessible covers and setting fiducials. A serviceable machine can last across generations while some parts are renewed; that differs from an untouched machine running for centuries.
- Default to a sealed/guarded exhibition with inspection windows and a safe walkway. An exploded/service cutaway changes the illustration, not permission to put hands into moving machinery. Isolation and supported-load scenes precede repair animation.
- Service intervals and lifetime are **TBD by evidence**. Story scenes may depict checks and care without asserting a real maintenance schedule. A decades/centuries goal needs documented materials, wear/aging tests, accelerated-test assumptions and long-term evidence; short browser runs cannot establish it.

## Engineering ledger and reserves in the art

For a future engineering-study model, record time-stamped work input, each store's energy, mechanical output and dissipated loss with `ΔEstored = Ein − Eout − Eloss`. Use SI units where a model is dimensioned and clearly named normalized units where it is not. Losses are nonnegative; no branch is credited twice; efficiencies cannot exceed one. Validate accounting against independent examples before art follows its state. Avoid frame-integrated simulation that changes when the tab slows.

The everyday clock's first scene may use deterministic authored charging/rest vignettes and a labeled qualitative reserve rather than claiming a calibrated energy simulation. If so, reserve bars are storytelling and cannot be cited as engineering conservation evidence. A later measured/parameterized study can replace that model behind the same object interfaces. A time jump reconstructs the clock scene; it does not imply unobserved people delivered unmeasured work or that a finite physical reserve never ran out.

Reserve gives the workers permission to stop, share food and watch the machine together. If a study exhausts its reserve, its mechanical train stops honestly; the observer's current-time panel continues from the device clock. Low reserve is not permission to skip rest or make a child work.
