## What must change

The current rig already has fixed-length upper/lower arms and legs, a two-link inverse-kinematics solver, selected planted routes, attached tools and deterministic motion. The missing detail is anatomical geometry and coordinated movement around those endpoints. Adding fingers to the existing wrist dots would leave the main mechanical problems unresolved.

## Anatomy contract

Keep identities, appearance and equipment separate from anatomy and activity. Profiles are shared defaults selected centrally, with explicit overrides for body shape/age where justified. A role chooses grips/stance and carried equipment; it must not redefine an identity's skeleton in a theme file.

Proposed record, not an implemented API:

```text
anatomyProfile: units, segments, jointFrames, jointLimitsByView,
                palms, feet, attachments, silhouetteDetail
poseFrame:     root, pelvis, chest, head, facing/view,
                arms, legs, handFrames, footFrames, contacts, props
contact:       surfaceId, mode, localSocket, worldTarget,
                surfaceNormal, tangent, phase, loaded
```

- **Units and transforms:** bone lengths use canonical rig units; local axes are +x right and +y down. Rotations use an explicit signed convention (radians in calculations, degrees in review data). Anatomical left/right belongs to the character, independent of screen direction. Every frame identifies rig, actor, camp/world and screen coordinates. Convert points and normals correctly through each transform; do not assume a uniform scale or that mirroring preserves handedness.
- **Skeleton:** pelvis/hip roots → thigh → knee → shin → ankle; chest/shoulder roots → upper arm → elbow → forearm → wrist. Fixed-length bones remain the measured skeleton beneath curved/tapered art. Head, shoulder seams, clothing, backpack, harness and belay loop follow their owning frames.
- **Hand:** wrist → palm center → knuckle line, with thumb side, finger reach, grasp center and support patch. Provide relaxed, open/support, cylindrical tool grip, pinch, cupped/bowl support and climbing-hold shapes. Fingers can be grouped at small sizes; the thumb and grip direction must still read. Gloves cover the same hand anatomy rather than replacing it with a dot.
- **Foot:** ankle → heel, ball and toe, with sole thickness and forward direction. A standing foot uses a sole surface; walking uses heel/flat/forefoot contacts; climbing may use a ball/toe edge. Boots/shoes wrap those landmarks. A flat ankle endpoint is not a flat sole.
- **Joint limits:** Stage A1 must record numeric minimum/maximum flexion, preferred bend and view-dependent shoulder/hip/ankle/wrist envelopes for each profile, together with reference poses. Check neutral, reach, crouch, kneel, swing and loaded poses. A signed bend must not flip at a near-straight limb. Relaxed poses keep a small bend rather than locking every joint at full extension.
- **Projection limits:** these are stylized two-dimensional/limited-depth characters. Screen elbow angles cannot be treated as measured human joint angles. Use explicit front/side/turned view conventions and authored projection envelopes; document foreshortening and occlusion. Avoid claiming medical or force-accurate biomechanics. The numeric envelopes must be reviewed in A1 before any global pose migration, rather than guessed per scene.
- **Compatibility:** retain `arms[i][0..2]` as shoulder/elbow/**wrist**, and `legs[i][0..2]` as hip/knee/**ankle**. Add palm/sole landmarks; do not silently redefine index 2 as a fingertip/toe. Preserve existing identities, role validation, contact keys and public methods until every consumer has migrated. Mark legacy endpoint contacts as legacy; they cannot count as new palm/sole acceptance.

If separated modules help keep responsibilities clear, introduce proposed `character-anatomy.js` (profiles, landmarks, constrained solving) and `character-motion.js` (shared contact/gait sampling), with `characters.js` retaining the public facade, identities, roles and renderer. Keep the current browser-script/pure-Node loading style. Module extraction must happen behind compatibility checks, not alongside an unrelated rewrite; register any new file in hosted, workshop, server and portable loading.

## Movement rules

### Hands, reaching and work

Reach begins with a small shoulder/chest preparation, followed by upper arm, forearm and wrist. Finger opening precedes contact; closure follows alignment. Release opens the grasp before the hand leaves. Avoid a uniform sine wave on all joints or a hand that rotates freely while the tool stays behind.

Use the **palm/tool grip frame**, rather than the wrist origin, as the visible contact. Given a target grasp and orientation, solve the wrist offset and then the arm. Keep a loaded grip aligned during breathing and secondary movement. Joint limits and a reachable working end must be satisfied together. Two-handed objects use two distinct reachable handle/support points; an impossible target triggers re-staging or rejection rather than stretched arms or detached hands.

Migrate the spoon/pot, gathered sticks/greens, map, bottle, food, shelter line, mallet/peg, rake, carried wood, firesteel, filter bottle, play stick and bowl/stand. Add grip metadata to currently rendered props first; the 83-item availability catalog does not imply 83 rendered tools. During bowl placement, match the actual rim/underside support to the tabletop before opening the hand. Preserve independent head and body support for Pebble using visible palm patches; do not merely move the infant to a new wrist midpoint.

### Feet, legs and travel

Separate a route's progression from the gait's contact schedule. A step has weight transfer, unloading, toe-off, swing/clearance, landing and settling. Ordinary walking has overlapping support around transfer; a supported pause begins before working. The stance landmark stays fixed in world space while the pelvis passes it. Heel-to-flat-to-ball/toe roll transfers load between distinct landmarks: keep the currently loaded pivot fixed until its explicit release, then use the next landmark's world position as the new fixed pivot. Do not require the heel and toe to occupy the same point. Knees flex with weight transfer; ankles adapt to the support normal; swing toes clear the ground.

Choose step count, length and duration from distance, slope, character proportions and carrying posture. Arm counter-swing yields to an occupied hand. Turns use deliberate foot placement and facing transitions instead of an instantaneous whole-body mirror. Uphill/downhill, stepping onto a raised foreground, kneeling, resting and shallow-water play need their own contact modes. Do not force a heel strike for every action: climbing, kneeling and careful edge steps use different support surfaces.

Candy's running can have an authored flight phase; its tests must distinguish running from grounded walking. Bike support comes from seat/handlebar/pedal contacts, with feet following the crank and knees remaining reachable, rather than treating pedals as ground or simply hiding the legs. Broom work needs a grip and a braced stance through the sweep.

Terrain supplies a surface/normal and authored clearance envelope. Check the sole and swing arc against those surfaces, hearth and nearby work props. This is bounded kinematic collision/clearance checking, not a new general terrain or rigid-body physics engine. When a route is infeasible, adjust its authored root/holds or reject it; clamping the rendered root after solving breaks contacts.

### Whole-body coordination and climbing

Apply story intent → contact schedule → feasible pelvis/chest shift → constrained limb solve → palm/sole/prop transforms → bounded secondary motion. Hard loaded contacts take priority over decorative breathing, sway or spring effects. A small head/backpack lag may follow the body, but no post-solve effect may drag a planted foot, tool grip or supported baby.

Grounded work uses a body-weight proxy over the available support interval in the selected view, with explicit crouch/lean bounds. Climbing uses its authored support contacts and harness attachment; a ground-only support-polygon test is inappropriate for a wall. Preserve three supports during one-limb rock transfers and supported recovery. Add hold normals, palm/boot contact orientation and reach checks; retain mandatory rest and deadline-only aid. Rope protection stays attached to the harness, and aid foot-loop attachment must name an appropriate boot/foot socket. Rope forces, pendulum simulation and full center-of-mass dynamics remain later work.

Motion must be deterministic from the sampled clock/activity time. Do not add frame-integrated springs that diverge with frame rate or replay missed work after sleep. Use smooth authored curves with continuous position and velocity at normal joins; prefer continuous acceleration where the action permits it. Distinguish deliberate contact events from accidental snaps. Fatigue can reduce free gesture amplitude and increase a supported pause, within the existing story budget; it must never delay current digits, skip recovery or invent persistent biology.

## Acceptance contract for future implementation

These are **proposed gates**, not measured results. Existing tests are valuable but currently establish endpoint lengths/contacts and selected joins; they cannot establish palm closure, sole roll, joint envelopes or natural motion without those landmarks.

1. **Geometry and constraints.** Enumerate legal identity/role/profile/action combinations and dense samples of turns/blends. Every bone is finite and length-preserving (retain existing `1e-8` rig-unit checks where appropriate). Check signed bend branches, angular envelopes and clearance/reach failures. Include near-extension singularities and mirrored/turned views. Unit fixtures must include externally specified expected landmarks, not only compare the solver with itself.
2. **Visible contacts.** Independently project actual painted palm, thumb/grip, heel, ball/toe, tool handle/tip, table and infant support points. Proposed screen tolerance: ≤0.5 CSS px for loaded palm/sole/prop contact error and stance drift relative to its moving surface; loaded infant head/body contacts retain the existing stricter <0.2 CSS px gate. Allow no unintended ground penetration beyond a ≤0.5 CSS px numerical/rendering tolerance. Measure in the surface frame, separating camera motion from slip. In A0 record existing endpoint, layout and cadence metrics. In A1 freeze the proposed new error budgets against independent reference geometry and projected sizes. With the first rendered landmarks in A2, measure residuals against those references before evaluating later motion candidates; the old renderer cannot supply a measured palm/sole baseline. Any justified budget revision needs recorded evidence and review before acceptance, never a change made to hide a candidate failure. Enlarged art must not count as native-size acceptance.
3. **Gait and support.** Assert ordered support events, expected heel/flat/toe or action-specific pivots, swing clearance, continuous root placement and feasible hard contacts. Sample enough interior frames to catch mid-step violations. The support test must inspect independent heel/ball/toe surfaces, not relabel the old ankle endpoint as a sole. Include a two-hand carry, kneel/stand, climb lift and first/last step. Ground body-weight proxy checks are kinematic heuristics, not force validation.
4. **Continuity.** Use fixed one-sided finite differences around joins (initial review samples ±1, ±4 and ±8 ms) for root, every joint, palm orientation, heel/toe and held objects. Report position residuals in rig units and CSS px, velocities in CSS px/s, angular velocities in degrees/s, and acceleration where applicable. Set per-action bounds from the approved reference motion in A1. Existing position checks remain. Ordinary loops and routine transitions require continuous position/velocity; explicit release/impact/facing events have named exceptions with visual review, not blanket exemptions. Cover meal stage/minute wraps, daily window ends, dawn/dusk, hour/day/DST changes, resize and resume.
5. **Perceived organic movement.** Review short continuous clips plus canonical still/contact sheets at actual smallest sizes and a larger inspection scale. Look for wrong thumb side, backward knees, stiff wrists, floating soles, simultaneous mirrored arm swings, hand clipping through a bowl/baby, detached sleeves/boots, overactive idle motion and abrupt facing changes. A numeric pass alone is insufficient. Humanlike variety comes from bounded role/profile timing and load-aware gestures, not random noise on every joint.
6. **Browser, layout and lifecycle.** Test hosted and portable in native Chrome, Firefox and Edge at 854×480, 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 390×844, DPR1/2. Include all seven identities, eligible theme overrides, bare/gloved hands and all active action families; use a representative browser matrix plus exhaustive pure profile tests. Retain the ≥30 CSS px independent family-group gap and ≥16 CSS px horizontal viewport margins, current glyph/controls/crop checks and infant support. Hands/feet enlarge painted bounds even when actor roots stay put. Preserve quiet/reduced-motion, hidden tab, Settings, screen rest, companions off and immediate current time after resume. Still camera only disables camera movement; retain the existing separate character-motion contract.
7. **Performance and device evidence.** Preserve all 66 existing/current cadence cases and their real 2.1-second windows/warmups: low power ≥26 updates/s, p95 ≤46 ms; normal ≥45 updates/s, p95 ≤31 ms; all max gaps <100 ms. Add active palm/grip, heel/toe and multi-character/climb/Candy probes measuring real rendered changes. Keep SVG nodes stable; avoid per-frame DOM queries/rebuilds and large finger/filter effects at tiny sizes. Simplify decorative finger detail by a deterministic projected-size rule if needed, while retaining contact landmarks. Record node/mutation growth and short-sample limits. Sustained Ally and Vanadium tests, including warm running, remain separate acceptance work; prior intermittent stalls are still unexplained.

Each implementation result must name source, test revision, portable checksum, suites actually run, failed/unrun cases, visual review and the remaining consumers. Add an expected-failing predecessor probe for each new behavior where useful (for example missing palm/sole geometry), so merely adding labels cannot satisfy acceptance.
